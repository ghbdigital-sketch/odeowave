Who we are

A young team
with fifteen years
of rooms behind it.

OdeoWave was founded by Puneet Kumarr after years of watching AV arrive too late on a project. We plan alongside the architect and the interior designer, not after the paint is dry, which is why our systems perform without being seen.

We are brand agnostic. No single manufacturer suits every room, so hardware is chosen once the acoustics, the use, the interiors and the budget are understood.
