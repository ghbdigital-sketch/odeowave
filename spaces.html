Spaces

Every room is a
different problem.

A speaker does not have a sound. A room does. Each space type gets its own coverage study, its own noise floor and its own control logic.

01 / Clubs and discos

Energy on the floor,
conversation at the bar.

The hardest brief in the business. The dance floor needs impact, the bar needs to stay conversational, and the building next door needs to hear nothing at all.

  • 01
    Line array and dance floor subs

    Directivity aimed at people, not at the ceiling.

  • 02
    Zoned bar and lounge audio

    Separate levels and sources per area.

  • 03
    DJ booth monitoring

    An isolated feed the DJ can actually work on.

  • 04
    Sharpy, wash, laser and haze

    DMX scenes sequenced with the music.

  • 05
    Limiter and isolation

    Set to the licence, so the lease survives the season.

02 / Lounges, bars and cafes

Atmosphere without
raised voices.

When guests have to shout, they leave early. Even coverage at low level, with the music present everywhere and loud nowhere.

  • 01
    Distributed ceiling and surface speakers

    Close spacing, low level, no hot spots.

  • 02
    Compact subwoofers

    Warmth under the music without rattling the glassware.

  • 03
    Scene control

    Open, service, peak hour and close, on one keypad.

  • 04
    Architectural lighting

    Colour temperature that drops as the evening moves.

  • 05
    Absorption in the fit out

    Hidden inside ceiling rafts and upholstery.

03 / Gyms and health clubs

Loud, even,
and contained.

Level at the squat rack has to match level on the cardio deck, the low end has to be felt, and the flat upstairs has to stay asleep.

  • 01
    High SPL distributed loudspeakers

    Zoned by activity area, rated for continuous duty.

  • 02
    Decoupled subwoofers

    Mounted on isolators so energy goes into the air, not the slab.

  • 03
    Studio systems

    Instructor microphone, ducking and independent control.

  • 04
    Multi zone control

    Source and volume per zone from the front desk or an app.

  • 05
    Isolation strategy

    Floating floors and decoupled mounts where the structure demands it.

04 / Home theatres and media rooms

An engineered
sensory environment.

Not a display with speakers around it. Seating, sightlines, treatment, lighting and sound are all set on the same drawing, before the joinery is cut.

  • 01
    Dolby Atmos and DTS:X layouts

    Bed and height layer positioned to the seating plan.

  • 02
    Acoustic treatment

    First reflection points, rear absorption and bass management.

  • 03
    Projection or direct view LED

    Screen sized to viewing distance, not to the wall.

  • 04
    Scene lighting and motorised curtains

    One keypad takes the room to movie mode.

  • 05
    Calibrated processing

    Time alignment and correction per seat.

06 / Auditoriums, campuses and worship

Every word,
to the last row.

Large volumes, hard surfaces and one job above all others. Intelligibility, not volume, is the specification.

  • 01

  • 02

  • 03
    Feedback elimination

  • 04
    Projection or LED wall

    Sized and positioned for the back row and the balcony.

  • 05
    Recording and streaming feed

    A clean broadcast mix, separate from the room mix.
