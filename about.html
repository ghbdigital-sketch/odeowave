Founder

Puneet Kumar.

Fifteen years in audio visual, most of it on site. OdeoWave exists because he kept being handed rooms where the AV had been decided last, after the ceiling was closed and the budget was spent.

The company runs on one principle taken from that experience. AV planning has to begin alongside spatial design, not after the interiors are finished.

Process

Six stages,
one responsibility.

From the first conversation to the annual service visit, the same team stays on the job.

01

Concept

Identify the need, the spatial goals and how the room will really be used.

02

Design

AV layouts, reflected ceiling plans and technical specifications against your drawings.

03

BOQ

A detailed proposal with options, priced line by line and open to question.

04

Execute

Site wiring, back boxes, rack building and mechanical installation to the site programme.

05

Commission

Testing, DSP tuning, programming and handover with training.

06

Support

Maintenance, remote support and scheduled system health checks.

How we specify

Brand agnostic,
on purpose.

We hold no allegiance to a single manufacturer. The room, the use and the budget decide the hardware, in that order.

01

The room first

Volume, surfaces, geometry and noise floor are measured or modelled before a single product is named.

02

The use second

A gym at peak hour, a boardroom with a guest presenter and a banquet hall all fail differently. We design for the failure mode.

03

The budget third

Options at three levels, with the trade off stated plainly, so you can spend where it is audible and save where it is not.
