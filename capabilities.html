05 / Acoustic design

Measured before
it is quoted.

Every specification starts with the room. Reverberation, reflection and coverage are modelled first, because no amount of equipment fixes a room that rings.

  • 01
    Acoustic modelling

    RT60 times, reflection points and absorption needs against room geometry.

  • 02
    SPL and coverage maps

    Sound pressure simulated across the listening zone before placement is fixed.

  • 03
    Treatment design

    Absorption, diffusion and isolation drawn into the interior package.

  • 04
    DSP tuning

    Time alignment, equalisation and feedback elimination, on site.
